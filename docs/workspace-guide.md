# Catering workspace

- **Catering:** import a CSV, check its preview, and assign a Cabin Services lead from the dashboard. Search or filter the run-status list to follow progress. Open Reconciliation to review and approve received reports.
- **Cabin Services:** find a run in Active or History. Use Team to assign Pursers, Consumption to check saved quantities, and Report to preview and send the final report to Catering. Sending closes Purser editing; downloading a preview does not send it.
- **Flight Purser:** open an assigned run, search or sort its items, enter consumed quantities, and save. Remaining quantities are calculated automatically. Add remarks when useful. History contains runs already sent for review.
- **Director:** review consumption, returns, approved costs, and category totals. Operational quantities and approved financial costs have separate labels.
- **Admin:** manage staff access, review operational counts, and search audit activity.

The notification bell shows assignments and report updates. Select an update to open its workspace, or mark all updates read.

On desktop, collapse the sidebar to give tables more room. On mobile, use the page shortcuts or open the menu for all navigation and Sign out. Consumption items become touch-friendly cards. Open full preview to read a report in a full-screen view.

Unsaved consumption is protected when switching runs, pages, signing out, or closing the tab. Choose Keep editing to save it first, or explicitly discard it.
